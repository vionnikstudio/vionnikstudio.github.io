## Features

| | |
|---|---|
| **Magnifier** | Click any pixel on your screen, even in another app, and the color goes straight into your swatches, ready to name. |
| **Live preview** | RGB and HEX values show in real time under the pointer. Sample sizes: 1×1, 3×3, 5×5, 9×9. |
| **Lists, categories, projects** | File a color in a list, a category and a project, and find it instantly by any of the three. |
| **Search and tags** | Name, usage, HEX, RGB or tag: a single search field filters your swatches as you type. |
| **Harmonies and contrast** | Complementary, analogous, triad, plus the WCAG contrast ratio (AA/AAA) with white or black text. |
| **Export** | JSON, Adobe swatches (.ase), macOS palette (.clr), 160 × 100 JPG image or a PDF sheet per project. |
