# Le Coloriste

**Pick a color. Anywhere.**

An eyedropper that works even when the app is in the background. Every color becomes a named swatch, organized by list, category and project, ready to export to Photoshop, macOS or a PDF color sheet.

**CA$9.99 · one-time purchase · coming soon to the Mac App Store**

macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later · Apple silicon and Intel · English and French

---

## Features

| | |
|---|---|
| **Magnifier** | Click any pixel on your screen, even in another app, and the color goes straight into your swatches. |
| **Live mode** | RGB and HEX values show in real time under the pointer. A global shortcut picks the color under the mouse. |
| **Organized swatches** | Lists, categories, projects and tags to find every named swatch. |
| **Harmonies** | Complementary, analogous, triad: one click adds the chosen harmony to your swatches. |
| **WCAG contrast** | Checks AA and AAA readability with white or black text. |
| **Exports** | JSON, Adobe swatches (.ase), macOS palette (.clr), 160 × 100 JPG image or a PDF sheet per project. |

## Details

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| Sample size | 1×1, 3×3, 5×5 or 9×9 pixels |
| Appearance | Light, dark or automatic |
| Sync | Across multiple Macs |
