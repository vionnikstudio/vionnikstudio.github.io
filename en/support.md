## Le Coloriste

**Current version:** 1.0 · **Requires:** macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later · Apple silicon and Intel

### Getting started

1. Open Le Coloriste and click the **magnifier**.
2. Click any pixel on your screen: the color is added to your swatches.
3. Give it a name and file it in a list, category or project.
4. Export your colors as JSON, Adobe (.ase), macOS (.clr), JPG or PDF.

### Frequently asked questions

**The magnifier doesn't pick colors from other apps.**
macOS may ask for your permission before Le Coloriste can read the screen. Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, find Le Coloriste in the relevant list, turn it on, then relaunch the app.

**Where are my colors stored?**
On your Mac. If you use sync, they are also kept in your own iCloud account so they're available on your other Macs.

**How do I get my colors on another Mac?**
Sign in to the same iCloud account on both Macs and turn on sync. You can also export a JSON file and import it on the other Mac.

**How do I install Le Coloriste on another Mac?**
Open the Mac App Store with the same Apple Account, click your name in the bottom-left corner, then download Le Coloriste from your purchases. No extra charge.

**How do I get a refund?**
Purchases are made through Apple, which handles refunds. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Are my colors sent to Studio Vionnik?**
No. Le Coloriste collects no data. See our [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy/).
