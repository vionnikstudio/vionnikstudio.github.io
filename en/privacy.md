## 3. In short

- **Le Coloriste runs on your Mac.** Your colors stay there. We have no server that receives them.
- **We don't create user accounts**, and we never sell, rent or trade personal information.
- **No ads, no ad trackers.**
- **Purchases go through Apple.** We never see your name, card or billing address.
- **If you email us**, we use your address only to reply, unless you agree to receive our news.

## 4. Le Coloriste

**What the app stores:** your colors, swatches and preferences, saved on your Mac. If you turn on sync, they are also synced across your Macs through your own iCloud account, under Apple's privacy policy. Studio Vionnik has no access to your iCloud.

**Screen access:** to pick a color from another app, macOS may ask you to allow Le Coloriste to read the screen. The app uses this only to read pixel colors, on your Mac. No image of your screen is saved or transmitted. You can revoke this permission at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

**What Studio Vionnik receives:** nothing.

## 5. App Store purchases and data provided by Apple

Le Coloriste is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, billing, taxes and refunds under its own privacy policy. **We receive neither your name, nor your address, nor your payment information.**

Apple provides us with:

- **aggregated sales statistics** (for example, the number of sales per country) that cannot identify you;
- **crash reports and anonymized usage data**, only if you chose to share them with developers in your Mac's settings.

We use this data only to fix bugs and improve our apps.

## 6. Website and email

**Website:** vionnik.com uses no advertising cookies and no tracking tools. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security purposes, under its own policy.

**When you email us:** we receive your email address, your name if it appears and the content of your message. We use them to reply and provide support.

**News and launches:** we send you news **only if you have given your express consent**, as required by Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL). Every message includes an unsubscribe link.

**Retention:** we keep support emails for [24 months] after the last exchange, then delete them. Your subscription to our news is kept until you unsubscribe.

## 9. Your rights

At any time, you can:

- **access** the personal information we hold about you;
- have it **corrected** if it is inaccurate or incomplete;
- **withdraw your consent**, for example by unsubscribing from our news;
- request that your information be **deleted**;
- obtain a copy of your information in a structured, commonly used technological format (**portability**).

Write to the person in charge (section 2). We reply in writing within **30 days**.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may file a complaint with the **Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec** ([cai.gouv.qc.ca](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca)).
